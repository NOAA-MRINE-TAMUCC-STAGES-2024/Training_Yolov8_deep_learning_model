# You will need your own statistical information for sightings, and R-Studio

Use tidyverse on R-Studio to determine when and where it is most statistically likely to find your object of interest. Make sure your latitude and longitude values are in +/- format, not NSEW, and your months are numerical (e.g. January=1, February=2, etc.)
